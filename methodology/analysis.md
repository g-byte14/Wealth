# Analysis — how you write "Here's What I See"

The analysis is a mirror and a map. The person should read the first line and
feel *seen*, then finish feeling *calm and clear about what to do on Monday*.

## Section guidance

**title** — A 2–5 word name for their situation, like a chapter title.
("The Founder Funnel", "Urgent Everything", "Two Businesses in One")

**happening** — What appears to be happening. The headline is one sentence that
reframes their mess in a way they haven't put into words yet. The detail is 2–3
sentences connecting the symptoms they described.

**bottleneck** — The single underlying constraint. Name it precisely. The detail
explains the mechanism: how this one thing produces the symptoms they feel.

**complexity** — What is creating unnecessary complexity. A headline plus 2–4
concrete items, each naming a specific thing from their description.

**eliminate / automate / delegate / system** — 1–3 items each. Each item has
`what` (the concrete thing, in their words) and `why` (one sentence). Be
specific: "the Monday status meeting", not "unnecessary meetings". If a category
genuinely has nothing, give one item explaining what to watch for instead.

**leverage** — The highest-leverage intervention. One move. The headline is the
move itself, stated as an imperative. The detail says what it looks like in
practice. `whyThisFirst` explains why this beats everything else on the list.

**nextActions** — Exactly three actions, in order. Each must be doable within a
week, start with a verb, and be concrete enough that they'd know when it's done.
Include a rough timeframe ("Today, 30 minutes", "This week", "By Friday").

**leverageMap** — Five short nodes telling the story of the change:
CURRENT STATE → BOTTLENECK → LEVERAGE POINT → NEW SYSTEM → DESIRED STATE.
Each node has a `label` (2–6 words) and a `detail` (one sentence). Read in
sequence, the five labels should form a coherent story on their own.

## Quality bar
- Every section must reference specifics from their description and answers.
- No section may contradict another; the bottleneck, leverage point and first
  next action should obviously connect.
- Prefer fewer, sharper words. If in doubt, cut.
